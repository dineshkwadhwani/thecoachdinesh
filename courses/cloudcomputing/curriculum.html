Course Curriculum

Cloud Security, CSPM & SSO

Two lectures · Four hours · One connected view of cloud configuration security and identity access.

Lecture 1 · 2 hours · Slides 1–15

Foundations of Cloud Security & CSPM

  • Cloud recap: IaaS, PaaS, SaaS, and public, private, and hybrid deployment models.
  • The shared responsibility model: how responsibility shifts across service models.
  • The Capital One breach as a case study in customer-side misconfiguration and excessive permissions.
  • Five cloud security pillars and common cloud threat patterns.
  • Cloud Security Posture Management: continuous discovery, assessment, prioritization, remediation, and monitoring.
  • Compliance frameworks including CIS Benchmarks, PCI-DSS, ISO/IEC 27001, RBI IT Framework, and India's DPDP Act.
  • CSPM, CWPP, CIEM, KSPM, and CNAPP: where each capability fits.
Lecture 2 · 2 hours · Slides 16–29

Single Sign-On & Authentication

  • Why SSO reduces password fatigue; the User, Identity Provider (IdP), and Service Provider (SP).
  • Authentication (who you are) versus authorization (what you may access).
  • SAML 2.0 assertions and the SP-initiated enterprise sign-in flow.
  • OAuth 2.0 delegated access, authorization codes, scopes, back-channel token exchange, and PKCE.
  • OpenID Connect as an identity layer over OAuth 2.0, including signed ID tokens and JWT claims.
  • Banking and Lenskart identity examples, token and consent risks, MFA, and protocol selection.
Learning Objectives

By the end of the course

Explain who secures what in IaaS, PaaS, and SaaS.
Describe why CSPM is a continuous operational cycle, not a one-time audit.
Differentiate CSPM, CWPP, CIEM, and KSPM in practical terms.
Trace a SAML flow and an OAuth authorization-code flow.
Explain why OAuth alone is not an authentication protocol and what OIDC adds.
Identify SSO risks and select mitigations such as MFA, short token lifetimes, and least privilege.
