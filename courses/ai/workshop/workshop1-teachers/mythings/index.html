Where Things Are Kept

Build a knowledge base for a voice assistant that answers "where is my …?" Three steps: list your storage places, list your things, then tap where each one lives.

1 Your storage places

Whatever you actually call them. Drawer 1, hall cupboard, loft, garage shelf. These become the answer options in the next step.

2 Your things

Comma separated. Delete what you don't own, add what's missing. Start a line with Category: to group items under a heading.

3 Who is this for?

Used in the finished document so the assistant sounds like it belongs to you. Both optional.
