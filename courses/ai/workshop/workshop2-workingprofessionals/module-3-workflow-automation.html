Module 3

Workflow automation

This module moves participants from ad hoc AI use to repeatable, scalable work systems. The goal is to reduce low-value effort and create workflows that save time without creating unnecessary complexity.

What this area covers

  • How to turn repetitive office tasks into AI-enabled workflow steps.
  • Approaches for email drafting, summary generation, and report preparation.
  • How to structure workflows for analysis, content creation, and task follow-up.
  • Ways to combine AI with templates, checklists, and review loops.
  • How to improve team productivity without over-automating every task.
  • Examples of document review, meeting prep, and support-based work patterns.
  • Design principles for automation that remain useful, transparent, and maintainable.
