Module 2

Prompting for work

This module focuses on translating everyday work tasks into AI-ready instructions. Participants learn how to write prompts that produce better research, drafts, summaries, and decision support without wasting time on trial and error.

What this area covers

  • How to define the role, goal, context, and desired output clearly.
  • Prompt patterns for research briefs, emails, reports, and summaries.
  • Ways to tighten outputs using desired tone, constraints, and review criteria.
  • How to ask for structured answers instead of vague prose.
  • Techniques for improving drafting quality and consistency across documents.
  • How to refine prompts through iteration and evaluation.
  • Practical examples for communication, analysis, and executive ready content.
