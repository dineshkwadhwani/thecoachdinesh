Workshop 2

AI for Working Professionals

Build practical AI capability for daily work: faster research, sharper writing, smarter decision support, stronger communication, and safer use of automation in professional environments.

Explore the workshop Download overview PDF Download slides
What professionals learn

How to use AI for workflow design, content creation, research synthesis, meeting productivity, and problem solving.

Format

Practical workshop with guided activities, live examples, and hands-on prompts.

Best for

Managers, consultants, educators, analysts, and knowledge workers seeking real-world AI leverage.

Workshop outcomes

Practical outcomes for work life

1. AI for daily productivity

Use AI for summarization, drafting, faster review cycles, and improved communication quality.

2. AI for delegated thinking

Turn repeated work into clear prompts, templates, and structured workflows that save time and reduce friction.

3. AI for decision support

Compare options, synthesize information, and create decision-ready briefs with evidence and trade-offs.

4. AI with responsibility

Learn guardrails, accuracy checks, privacy awareness, and how to keep human judgment in the loop.
Learning tracks

Workshop structure

Module 1 · Foundations

How AI works in work contexts, what it is good at, and where it can fail.

Module 2 · Prompting for work

Prompt patterns for research, drafting, analysis, review, and communication.

Module 3 · Workflow automation

Build repeatable AI-enabled workflows for emails, summaries, reports, and support tasks.

Module 4 · Responsible use

Use AI responsibly with verification, governance, privacy, and quality checks.
