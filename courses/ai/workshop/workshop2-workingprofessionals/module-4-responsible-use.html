Module 4

Responsible use

AI is most useful when it is used with discernment. This module supports a healthy, realistic approach to adoption by emphasizing review, governance, privacy, and clear human accountability.

What this area covers

  • How to verify AI outputs before sharing or acting on them.
  • Best practices for privacy, data handling, and caution with sensitive content.
  • How to create human-in-the-loop review processes for important decisions.
  • Ways to avoid over-reliance on AI in high-risk or highly contextual work.
  • How to design quality checks for accuracy, tone, and relevance.
  • Governance principles for teams adopting AI responsibly at work.
  • When to use AI as a collaborator, and when to keep human judgment central.
