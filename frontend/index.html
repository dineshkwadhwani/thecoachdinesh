My Journey

My leadership journey began as a software engineer passionate about building technology products. Over the past two decades, I have grown through multiple leadership roles in global organisations, leading distributed engineering teams and delivering enterprise platforms used by millions.


As I moved into senior leadership roles, I discovered that leadership success was not just about technical capability. It required clarity of thinking, the ability to influence stakeholders, and the ability to develop other leaders.

Engineering Leader
Leading global engineering teams and complex technology platforms, delivering innovative solutions with emerging technologies while mastering people dynamics in high-stakes environments.
Senior Director
Driving product and platform strategy across large organisations. Orchestrating strategic initiatives and fostering a culture of innovation and excellence.
Leadership Coach
Leadership Coach specializing in executive development, empowering leaders to unlock their potential and achieve transformative growth.
